# RepeatRemind — Concept & Vision

> *"The reminder app for things that don't happen every day."*

---

## The Problem

Standard reminder apps (Google Calendar, iPhone Reminders, Samsung Reminder) are built for two patterns:
- **Fixed date** — "Remind me on June 15"
- **Simple recurrence** — "Every day / Every week / Every month"

But real life has a third pattern that nobody handles well:

> *"Remind me again in 3 months."*
> *"Remind me after 5,000 km."*
> *"Remind me every 2 seasons."*

These are **interval-based recurring tasks** — and they're everywhere:

| Task | Interval |
|---|---|
| Car oil change | Every 5,000–10,000 km |
| Descale coffee machine | Every 2–3 months |
| Replace water filter | Every 6 months |
| Air conditioner filter | Every 3 months |
| Flea/tick treatment for pets | Every 4 weeks |
| Rotate car tyres | Every 10,000 km |
| Check fire extinguisher | Every 12 months |
| Fertilize houseplants | Every 2–4 weeks |
| Dental check-up | Every 6 months |
| Backup important files | Every 2 weeks |

Standard reminder apps make users manually reschedule each time. Most people just forget — or feel vague anxiety that they've forgotten *something* they were supposed to maintain.

---

## The Solution

**RepeatRemind** is a dedicated app for recurring maintenance tasks with custom intervals. Set it once, and it automatically reschedules every time you mark it done.

The core user experience is:
1. **Add a task** with a name, category, and interval (days / weeks / months / km)
2. **Get notified** when it's due
3. **Mark as done** — the app automatically calculates and sets the next due date
4. **Never manually reschedule** again

## Core Design Philosophy

- **Zero friction onboarding** — Add a task in under 30 seconds. No sign-up.
- **Works offline** — No cloud dependency. Everything lives on device.
- **Calm UI** — Not alarming. Gentle nudges, not anxiety-inducing red badges.
- **Set and forget** — Once configured, runs silently in the background.

---

## Target Users

**Primary:** Adults 25–45 who own a car, home, or pets and care about maintenance
**Secondary:** Anyone managing shared household tasks with a partner
**Tertiary:** Small business owners managing equipment maintenance

---

## App Store Position

**Category:** Productivity → Utilities
**Keywords:** maintenance reminder, recurring task, interval reminder, home maintenance, repeat reminder, pet reminder
**Competing search terms:** "home maintenance app", "recurring reminder", "pet medication reminder", "appliance maintenance tracker"

**Launch angle (homeowners first):** Lead with home + pet + health categories. km/mileage support is a Phase 2 feature — do not advertise at launch. Car-owner users will find the app organically; establish homeowner base first.
