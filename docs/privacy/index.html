RepeatRemind Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Data storage

RepeatRemind stores all data locally on your device only. No personal data is collected, transmitted, or shared with third parties.

Account

No account or registration is required to use RepeatRemind.

Network

RepeatRemind makes no network requests. All reminders and task data remain on your device.

Permissions

RepeatRemind requests notification permission to deliver maintenance reminders. No other sensitive permissions are required.
